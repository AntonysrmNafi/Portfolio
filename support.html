03 / Why

What this funds

BlockVeil began as a fix for support and communication problems I kept running into with Telegram-based tools. It has since grown into a small family of modular bots and independent products, built alone and maintained the same way. No investors, no ad revenue, just people like you deciding it is worth keeping alive.

04 / Impact

Where your contribution goes

Every contribution is split with care. Part of it goes directly to people who need it most: elderly people living without support, and children growing up in extreme poverty. Another part goes into planting trees. The rest goes back into BlockVeil and my other projects, keeping servers running, maintaining what already exists, and building new products and features. It is a small circle, but it holds together. A little bit of what you give means a tree stays rooted in the ground and someone, somewhere, breathes easier because of it. It means an elderly person gets to rest without worry. It means a child gets a slice of childhood that is not swallowed by hardship, and a real chance at an education. And it means the tools you already rely on keep running, and the next ones get built.
