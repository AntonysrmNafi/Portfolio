04 / Projects

My Projects

BLOCK #001 11 December 2025

BlockVeil (AES-256 Encryption Tool)

A simple tool for locking up a piece of text so only the right person can read it. Most encryption tools online quietly send your text to a server first, this one never does, everything happens right inside your browser and nothing ever leaves your device. Lock it with a password or with a secret key, watch your password strength as you type, and the result stays blurred until you choose to reveal it. Leave it alone for five minutes and it wipes itself clean, just to be safe.

JavaScript Web Crypto API AES-256-GCM

BLOCK #002 1 April 2026

BlockVeil Wallet Generator

A Telegram bot that creates crypto wallets in bulk, hundreds or thousands at once, the proper way: each one is generated using the same standard method real wallets use, not random guesswork. The results come back as a CSV file or as plain messages in chat, ready to use. Getting in needs an access code, usage runs on a simple balance system, and every wallet phrase is encrypted before it's stored anywhere. There's also a full admin side behind it for issuing codes and keeping track of everything. This one stays private though, it was never meant to go public.

Python eth-account AES-256-GCM
Private project, not publicly released
BLOCK #003 1 May 2026

BlockVeil Authenticator

A Telegram bot that keeps your two-factor authentication codes safe and easy to reach, right inside Telegram. Every account gets its own private vault, locked behind a password and a master key that even the server never sees in plain form. On top of that: encrypted offline backups, a way to share one code with someone for a few minutes without ever revealing the real secret, and a full admin side with premium plans and crypto payments built in.

Python SQLite AES-256-GCM
Live Demo
BLOCK #004 1 May 2026

BlockVeil QR Scanner & Generator

A Telegram bot that turns your chat into a quick QR and barcode reader, no commands needed, just send a photo or tap a button. It reads almost anything, wifi logins, contact cards, crypto addresses, payment codes, and lays the result out clearly instead of just dumping raw text. Short links get expanded and checked for phishing before you tap them. It can also turn any text into a QR code on the spot, and keeps your scan history encrypted and only in memory, nothing ever gets written to disk.

Python OpenCV AES-256-GCM
